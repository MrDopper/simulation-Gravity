##Objective: 
This project will help everyone understand more about gravity 


What is **gravity**? gravity is one of the well know force on Earth, orginally found by Newton. Gravity is the force exerted on an object in order to pull it towards another object, such as center of the Earth. 

Based on Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation

Newton described the forces of gravity between two bodies is directly proportional to the product of the two masses and inversely porportional to the product of the square of the distance between them. 
Essentially, the larger the objects get, the harder those pull. Additionally, the inversed square means that whenever the distance (R) increase, the pull of gravity is halved. If the distance between. two bodies is doubled, the pull of gravity will become one-fourth as strong. 

**What is the Gravitational Formula?**
Newton's Gravitational Law: 
$F =G\frac{M_1 M_2}{R^2}$
